WHAT THE CONTROL TARGETS
  The sweep claims to measure the decimator's OWN persistent state.
  If the measured 'settled amplitude' were an artifact of the harness
  (the stimulus, the block loop, the state-recurrence bookkeeping)
  rather than of the filter, then removing the filter's state
  persistence would NOT change it.  The control therefore zeroes
  bx/by/ax/ay at every 64-sample block boundary and REQUIRES every
  measured settled amplitude to become exactly 0.  It is two-sided:
  it also FAILS when the uncontrolled sweep contains no non-zero
  settled amplitude at all, because driving zero to zero shows
  nothing.
