Per running voice per block: one qmul per md route row (6 rows on the
carrier: 5 velocity + 1 keytrack; 8 rows on the synthetic class-cover
fixture), plus the keytrack word itself: ONE integer divide-by-6 of a
shifted key difference, no multiply, no table.

State: 1 x 32-bit keytrack word per voice instance (per-instance, never
shared) + a fixture-constant route ROM of 3 words per route. No
smoother, no history: the source is a pure function of key, scene
octave and keytrack root, so a voice's word is written once at note-on
(and re-derived at the end of each control pass, which is why the RTL
can recompute it from the streamed key instead of storing it).

Context (recorded, not reconciled): the SXT-022/SXT-034 voice datapath
measures 37.7-41.3 MAC/sample on comparable fixtures, so the keytrack
route pass is ~0.5% of the voice path on this carrier.
